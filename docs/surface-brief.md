# Bill workspace
Mode: Operate. Scope: home, separate /bills list, item editor, sharing, individual payments, authentication and account settings (nickname and PromptPay).
Authentication: the home introduction, demo and published shared bills are public; private shares require the bill PIN. Shared amount explanations use a participant-scoped token without requiring login. Bill creation, personal bills, account settings and payment pages require login and a complete username and nickname. Payment data, uploads and slip images retain account and participant-token checks. Public home actions lead to login before creating a real bill.
Primary task: add participants and items, assign participants, verify per-person amounts, save and share. Visible status must distinguish local draft, persisted draft, published bill, pending slip and verified payment.
Direction: light shared-expense ledger, deep green actions, item-level participant controls and a receipt summary that updates immediately. The home page's small example receipt demonstrates the mechanism and is labeled as illustrative.
Constraints: independent repo, independent database, existing bill arithmetic and payment protections. Production domain bill.wonglao.com is a target, not a deployment claim. No migrated data or production credentials are assumed.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Thai shared-expense ledger that keeps the item-to-person relationship visible while an organizer checks the real payment amount.
OWN-WORLD: Light white/gray paper, deep green actions, Thai Noto Sans with Manrope numerals, consistent stroke icons, dashed receipt subtotals and restrained participant colors.
STORY: Start a bill → add everyone including yourself → enter items → assign participants → verify per-person totals → save and share → transfer the exact outstanding amount and submit a slip.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home offers a new bill with a clearly labeled illustrative receipt and guidance. Personal bills, My bills/To pay tabs, search and the empty state live only on /bills, reached through the My Bills navigation item. Desktop editor puts the live receipt beside the first inputs; mobile retains a bottom total/summary shortcut as the fields scroll.
FORM: A continuous shared-expense ledger: compact item rows, participant selection attached to each item, right-aligned decimal amounts and a running receipt. Responsive stacking preserves the item-to-person grouping. No photographic assets or mock device frames are required for this working surface.

My Bills header: keep the title/subtitle and non-wrapping create action inline with a 20px gap above 760px; at 760px and below, stack the text above a full-width action with a 16px gap, preserving the existing copy and type scale.

My Bills status and To pay: show publication state separately from payment state (unpaid, partial, review, bank pending, paid or no payment). Created bills keep their full totals and settled-person counts. The counted To pay tab includes only positive outstanding balances on published bills linked to the session account or its aliases; unlinked names are never matched by spelling. Its amounts are personal outstanding totals. Keep private shares behind the existing PIN flow, aggregate historical aliases per bill and cap payments separately by recipient. Poll quietly through the existing live-refresh behavior while preserving active filters and search; show only My bills and To pay, with the two tabs side by side on compact screens.

Evidence: direction seed roll 01c1125c ran during setup. The user asked for a recommendation; the main thread proposed and implemented the ledger direction. There is no user-approved generated comp or quality-bar reference, and no exemption from the concept process is claimed. Design review assesses this built direction against the functional brief and craft floor.

Account setup: require both username and nickname before protected account or payment workflows. Prefill an existing immutable username as read-only; keep the nickname editable and required at 1–40 characters, with field-level errors. Save the completed setup once and return to the requested workflow. The header nickname opens /settings#nickname, where nickname and recipient details share a single save action. PromptPay may remain unconfigured. The stored nickname is used for shared creator/payment display.

Readability: ordinary body, form labels, inputs and primary buttons use 1rem (16px); helper text uses 0.9375rem (15px), compact metadata no smaller than 0.875rem (14px), and form headings 1.25rem (20px). Desktop and mobile share these reading floors; rem units respect browser font preferences.

PIN entry: exactly four visible digits in four equal boxes, shared by creation/editing and the shared-bill gate. Support numeric keyboards, focus advancement, whole-code paste, backspace correction and leading zeros. Server validation uses the same four-digit contract; existing stored PINs are not changed automatically.

Item payers: new bills show separate consumers and advancing-payer controls per item. The selected payer's PromptPay is shared across their items and edited through an explicit Add/Edit button opening a compact modal dialog. Keep numeric entry out of the inline item row; save commits and cancellation discards local edits. Receipt totals show consumption, advances, outgoing and incoming payments separately, followed by exact debtor-to-recipient transfers. Derive refunds from each item's payer and offset only reciprocal payments within the same pair; third-party debts keep their original recipients. Payment recipient selection switches the QR, slip evidence and owner review as one unit; no outgoing debt means no transfer action. Old bills retain the original recipient behavior until explicitly switched before evidence is submitted.

Brand: the visible logo says only bill, with a custom two-tone lowercase b mark. No Thai brand name or slash divider. Use the matching favicon, a 44px header link target, and compact 36px/40px SVG sizing. Preserve navigation and account behavior.

## Account connections in settings

This is an Operate extension of the existing settings surface, within its 650px content width and green/mint visual language. Keep the account profile form and the connections section distinct. The provider list shows Google and LINE connection status, an explicit connection action for an available unlinked provider, and an unavailable state when the provider is not configured.

After provider verification, show the profiles for review with provider names, display name, username, PromptPay and bill/group counts. For an existing second account, use a native radio group with no default primary selection. Selecting a profile announces which account will be primary and clears prior consent. Require a separate consent checkbox before enabling confirmation. A newly linked identity keeps the current profile without presenting a false choice.

Explain before confirmation that both sign-in identities are retained, existing bills/groups/relationships remain accessible, and participant balances and saved receiver snapshots remain separate. State that the old sessions end and a new sign-in is required. Keep cancel available during review. If confirmation reports expired proof, changed account data or a provider conflict, disable the stale choice and consent, clear the selection, and offer Start over to return to fresh verification. Preserve Thai/English copy, native keyboard control and visible focus within the existing settings layout; this extension does not introduce new design-system tokens.

## Signed-in payment and participant confirmation

Keep the existing shared receipt and amount dialogs useful before login. Amount explanations use the safe expenses response, with no receiver, slip or account details. Opening payment leads through login and, when necessary, the combined username/nickname setup before returning to that participant.

For a manually typed, unlinked participant, show the bill name, saved participant name, aggregate remaining amount, per-recipient remaining balances and an initially collapsed compact item/cost breakdown below the confirmation. Show prior confirmed payments where present and distinguish costs, advances, amounts payable and receivable. Keep QR/upload controls and private evidence reads out of the non-owner preview. Keep the confirmation heading, explanation, signed-in nickname/username and action together above the amounts. Emphasize the saved participant name in the explanation with bold dark-green text. Use the explicit “Confirm this entry is mine” action before exposing payment controls to a non-owner. Explain that linking switches the displayed name to the account nickname while preserving receiver details and balances. Keep the original typed label stored for history; display the primary linked account’s current nickname consistently in receipts, editing, payment and expense details, including consumers, payers and recipients. Unlinked entries keep their typed names, and account relations stay out of public receipt and expense responses. Explain that confirmation also connects the payer and bill owner as friends automatically. The link, friendship and audit commit together; subsequent visits use the account link. Do not infer identity from matching names. Explain a different-account or duplicate-account conflict and keep a route back to the shared bill. Owners can continue managing other participants without claiming them. If a signed-in non-owner already has a linked entry in the same bill, show other unlinked entries as read-only previews with an explicit notice and Open my entry action, never another claim prompt. The action opens their linked entry with a scoped participant token; keep claim and payment controls out of the other entry. Owners never see a claim action for other participants. A different signed-in account with a valid participant share/PIN token sees a read-only preview of amounts and items instead of a full-page account error. Use the participant’s name for costs and advances; hide claim, QR, upload and review actions, and omit receiver numbers and evidence from the response. Preserve canonical owner and linked-payer privileges. Preserve Thai/English copy, mobile stacking and the established green/mint settings and receipt language; this is an inherited surface extension with no new design tokens.

Participant picker: use one editable combobox for friends, saved groups, exact account lookup and manual names. Opening it shows accepted friends and groups with remaining members; typing filters them and automatically searches a valid exact username/email after a 400ms pause. Show a matched account directly, with no Search action; accepted friends appear immediately. Reuse normalized lookup results, abort stale requests, and offer explicit add-by-name only after no account matches. Enter adds a resolved account, never a manual name while lookup is pending or failed. Exclude canonical account IDs already selected, reset after adding, and keep group/friend loading and retries independent. Enter/arrow keys, Escape and outside blur operate the same picker; account identifiers never silently become manual guests. Preserve disabled/locked states and guest-only demo behavior, with one manage-friends link in the menu. No separate friend select, account disclosure or manual-name form.

Item detail controls: keep the discount input, Service charge and VAT on one row on mobile. Align labels and control boundaries with a shared grid, retain an 80px minimum discount field and 44px minimum targets, and allow Service charge to wrap inside its button on narrow screens without moving the button to another row.


Stopping sharing: the creator’s edit page offers Stop sharing beside the status even when payment evidence locks financial fields. Save only UNSHARED and a new version, preserve unsaved local fields and show a stopped-state notice; Share again remains in the existing summary action area. Shared receipt and participant URLs return 404 for everyone while stopped, and open receipts switch to the branded receipt-style 404 on refresh without revealing bill data. Keep the owner edit route available, route stopped created-list rows to it, and exclude stopped bills from To pay. Resume a payment-locked bill using only validated metadata, preserving its financial snapshot and PIN protections.


Canonical routes: use /:id for shared bills, /:id/:pid for payments and /manage/:id for editing. Keep tracked legacy route wrappers and 308 redirects for old URLs, preserving query strings. Every generated/share/QR/navigation/callback URL uses the shared path builders; session-storage keys and signed-token identities stay unchanged. Restrict public root recognition to CUIDs so account and management routes remain protected. Preserve PIN, profile, owner, read-only and stopped-bill checks, with a pre-stream HTTP 404 rewrite to the branded receipt-style not-found surface when necessary.

Branded 404: preserve true HTTP 404 for missing/stopped routes and show no bill data. Reuse the light mint canvas and white cut-bottom receipt paper, with a dark-green 404 inside one h1 and localized Thai/English copy explaining an incorrect link or stopped sharing. Keep the 6rem status display scoped to this surface, alongside the 24px title and 16px body. Primary My Bills and secondary Home links use two columns above 520px and stack at 520px and below, with 44px-plus targets and visible keyboard focus; OS dark preference retains the light canvas.

Shared receipt identity: mark participants linked to the signed-in account or its retained account aliases with a bold unboxed “You” identity label beside the name, a 20px green-dark name and a green-soft row background, and stably place those rows first. Keep this identity treatment distinct from filled payment-status badges; retain readable dark-green secondary text on the tinted row. Keep the other participants in their original order. Match account links only; names and bill ownership do not identify a personal participant row. Anonymous viewers and accounts without a linked entry see no personal marker. This ordering is presentation-only: preserve saved participants, rounding, balances, summary images and API order. Personalized receipt responses remain private and uncached.
