# เคลียร์โต๊ะ · Clear Toh
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Users
People splitting shared expenses such as travel, accommodation, household purchases and meals; the organizer assigns items and shares payment links.
## Product Purpose
A standalone bill-only project for bill.wonglao.com, with its own Git repository and MySQL database, explicitly requested by the user.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Create drafts, assign shared or individual items, discounts, service charge, VAT, per-person totals, item-level advancing payers with participant PromptPay numbers, item-payer reimbursements across multiple recipients with reciprocal offsets only within the same pair, private PIN-protected sharing, PromptPay, and verified slip uploads with owner review. The home page introduces bill creation; the personal bill list, My bills/To pay tabs, search and empty state are on the separate /bills page. Created-bill rows distinguish draft/shared/cancelled state from payment progress: unpaid, partial, awaiting slip review, awaiting bank confirmation, fully paid or no payment due. The To pay tab lists only published bills with a positive remaining balance for participant accounts linked to the current primary account or its aliases, including confirmed manual entries. Its amount is the current user’s aggregate outstanding balance, not the whole bill total. Archived, unlinked-name-only, cancelled, other owners’ drafts and fully paid entries do not appear in this tab. Multiple aliased entries are grouped per bill, and overpayments never offset a different recipient. Opening a row still uses the existing shared-bill/PIN flow. The home introduction is public. Bill creation, personal bills, account settings and owner changes require login. Published shared bills and safe amount explanations remain available without login: private shares require the bill PIN, and participant expense explanations require a signed token scoped to that participant and current bill revision. Payment pages, full payment data, uploads and slip images require login and a complete username and nickname; non-owner payment access also requires the participant-scoped token and the matching linked account. Draft/cancelled bills remain owner-only. Home, login, OAuth/session endpoints, static assets and the machine health endpoint are public. PIN and participant tokens remain additional authorization requirements after login. Bill PINs contain exactly four visible digits, entered in four boxes in both the editor and the shared-bill gate. Local drafts are scoped to the authenticated account. Users must set an account nickname (1–40 characters) independently of their OAuth name and PromptPay to complete account setup. Nicknames are used in the account header and shared bill/payment creator display, persist across sessions, and can be edited through account settings. Nickname and PromptPay share one form and one save action; a new account may leave PromptPay unconfigured. Keep authentication and ledger checks. No game, trip-planning, bot, or console features. Expense bills may include travel costs. No dependency on the WongLao repository or its database. Existing accounts and bills are not migrated implicitly.
## Brand Commitments
Public brand: เคลียร์โต๊ะ / Clear Toh, explicitly renamed by the user. Use a simplified friends-around-a-table symbol based on the user’s supplied visual reference, in the app’s green/mint palette, with Thai primary and English secondary wordmarks. The user approved the existing text treatment; preserve it when changing the symbol. Internal project/domain remains WongLao Bill. Thai-first interface with light surfaces and deep green.
## Evidence on Hand
Billing and payment implementation adapted from WongLao. No invented production data or live deployment claims.

Reimbursement rule: allocate each item to its actual advancing payer, then offset only reciprocal payments between the same two people. Never move a third person's refund to a different recipient. A participant may both owe and receive money; show both amounts and require PromptPay for anyone with an incoming transfer.

## Languages

Thai is the default and English is the second language. The TH/EN language dropdown is always on the navbar, for signed-in users and guests, separate from the account menu. Selection updates client translations immediately, saves a one-year browser cookie and refreshes server copy/metadata without changing the current URL or remounting unaffected client state. Page URLs have no language prefix. Old /th and /en links redirect to clean URLs with queries intact. API URLs, bill IDs, participant tokens and stored user data remain unchanged. Preserve user-entered names/items and the approved brand wordmark.

## Automatic payment updates

Published shared bills and participant payments refresh automatically about every three seconds while visible, with pause/abort during payment actions and background tabs, immediate retry on focus/reconnect, and capped failure backoff. Reads retain PIN and participant-token authorization. Only incorrect PIN guesses consume the PIN attempt quota; repeated valid reads do not. Remaining amounts reflect verified/confirmed ledger credits, not pending slip uploads.

Shared receipts support choosing a participant to show only their expense rows and balance, with an Everyone reset. Users can preview and share/download a receipt PNG with participant balances and a QR opening the bill. Confirmed completion adds a derived “เคลียร์แล้ว” stamp with reduced-motion support. Owners can reuse active participant names for a new empty round, with separate draft storage and no copied payment history.

Update: the separate name-picker strip was removed at the user’s request. Participants open their own expense/payment view by tapping their existing row; no duplicate name controls appear above the receipt.

Account setup and social groups: signed-in users missing a valid username or nickname must complete both before using owner/account features or payments. Onboarding prefills an existing username as read-only and requires a nickname of 1–40 characters; the profile API rejects updates that leave setup incomplete. Usernames normalize to lowercase ASCII letters, numbers and underscores, are 3–24 characters, unique, and immutable once set; display nicknames remain editable. Public shared bills and safe amount explanations remain available without account setup; payment and evidence access require the completed profile. Exact username or full-email search returns a minimal profile and relationship state, never email or PromptPay numbers. Manual friend requests require recipient acceptance. Saving a bill or owned group with linked accounts automatically connects the creator to those accounts; it does not connect every pair of group members. Saved groups seed new empty rounds using active account details and separate drafts. Linked participant names and PromptPay numbers are resolved from the account by the server at save time; other users cannot override them. PromptPay is self-configured, without an ownership-verification claim. Existing bills retain linked-account receiver snapshots until an eligible save; no payment history is rewritten by profile changes.

Auditable balances: tapping a published person’s amount opens their expense components, upfront payments, net overview and remaining transfers. Per-recipient details show exactly which paid items created the debt, which reverse-direction items offset it, confirmed payments and the remainder. Offsets stay within the same two people. Base/service-charge/VAT attribution and explicit satang adjustments explain existing ledger amounts without changing them; verified overpayment is shown separately and is never credited to another recipient.

The public home page includes an illustrative interactive receipt for equal sharing, per-item sharing and multiple advancing payers. Visitors can explore sample calculations without signing in; the examples use the existing settlement engine and do not save bills. Original creation/demo routes remain unchanged, and the page explains the workflow from adding people to sharing a guest-accessible bill link, signing in before payment and tracking confirmed completion.

## Connected sign-in accounts

Account settings let users connect Google and LINE after verifying the additional sign-in identity. If that identity already belongs to another account, the user explicitly chooses the primary account and confirms consent before merging. The primary account retains its username, display name and account PromptPay; both connected identities can subsequently sign in to it. A new identity can also be linked without merging an existing account. Conflicting identities for the same provider are rejected.

Merging retains source user records as aliases through `mergedIntoId`. Existing bills, groups and relationships resolve those aliases; financial, participant and payment rows are not rewritten. Two participant rows in the same bill remain distinct, with their original balances and saved receiver snapshots. Owner-only account-level PromptPay follows the primary profile, while saved participant receiver details remain governed by the existing bill-save rules. Starting a connection requires a login within 15 minutes. The verified proof is valid for 10 minutes, confirmation is single-use and revokes old sessions through `authVersion`; users sign in again after completion. Expired, changed or conflicting reviews require starting over with fresh verification.

## Participant identity before payment

A manually entered participant name does not identify an account. Before paying, a signed-in user with a complete profile can review the bill name, assigned items and expense breakdown, aggregate remaining payment, recipients and any prior payments, then explicitly confirms “Confirm this entry is mine” once. The non-owner preview does not show QR/upload controls or read private slip images. The server links that entry to the actual session account using the participant-scoped token, a same-origin POST and a bill lock. Successful linking also connects the payer and the bill owner as accepted friends, resolving a merged owner to the primary account and skipping self-friendship. The account link, friendship and audit commit atomically. Linking preserves the saved participant name, receiver details and all financial balances. It rejects another account's entry, a second active entry for the same account in the bill, archived entries, cancelled bills and stale bill versions; merged-account aliases count as the same account. Owners retain management access to other participants without automatically claiming those identities. A complete signed-in account opening another linked participant’s valid share/PIN-derived token receives a read-only amount and item view instead of an account-mismatch error. That response omits receiver numbers, evidence and account identities, and does not authorize claims, uploads or review actions. Ownership remains tied to the stored account and its merged aliases; matching names do not confer ownership.

The shared receipt's amount dialogs use a separate safe expenses endpoint that returns only expense explanations and settlement summaries, with the same calculation as the private payment view. It exposes no receiver details, slip evidence or account data. This access change uses the existing schema and requires no migration.


## Stopping bill sharing

The owner, including a merged owner alias, can stop sharing a published bill from the edit page even when payment evidence locks financial edits. This changes only sharing state to UNSHARED and increments the bill version; amounts, participants, settlements and payment history are retained. The shared receipt and participant page return 404 for a stopped bill, including for its owner, and existing receipt tabs replace their content with the 404 view on refresh. The owner edit API remains available. Stopped bills remain in the created list with an edit link and do not appear in To pay.

The owner can share again from the editor. A payment-locked bill resumes from its saved financial snapshot through a metadata-only update. Drafts still require complete bill data before first publishing; they cannot become UNSHARED to bypass validation. Stop/resume retain owner, version and private-PIN checks, and old participant tokens and stale payment targets remain invalid after the version changes. No schema migration is required because bill status is stored as a string.


## Canonical bill URLs

Shared receipts use /:id, participant payments use /:id/:pid, and creator editing uses /manage/:id. Existing /bill/:id, /bill/:id/:pid and /bills/:id/edit URLs permanently redirect with their query strings intact. Shared links, receipt-image QR codes, payment links, login/setup return paths, list rows and edit links use the canonical builders. Bill IDs and scoped payment tokens do not change. Only CUID receipt paths are public; manager/payment/account pages retain authentication and profile requirements, and private receipts retain PIN authorization. A small pre-stream status check returns a true HTTP404 with the standard not-found UI for absent, stopped or unauthorized unpublished receipts; stopped participant pages are also404.
